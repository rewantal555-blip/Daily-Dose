About Us

Daily Dose was founded with a simple idea: great coffee and pastries can brighten anyone's day. Our baristas are passionate about crafting the perfect cup of coffee, and our bakers take pride in creating delicious pastries from scratch. We source our ingredients sustainably, ensuring that every sip and bite supports ethical practices.

Our Story

Daily Dose started as a small neighborhood café with a mission to provide a welcoming space for coffee lovers. Over the years, we have grown into a beloved community hub, known for our quality beverages and friendly service. We believe that every cup of coffee tells a story, and we invite you to be part of ours. Our story is one of passion, dedication, and a love for the art of coffee making. We are committed to creating memorable experiences for our customers, one cup at a time.
